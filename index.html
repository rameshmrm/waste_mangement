How It Works — Smart City Waste Architecture

Real-time IoT fill monitoring reduces fuel consumption by up to 35% and prevents overflow bin emergencies.

1
📡 Collect Waste Data
Ultrasonic IoT sensors emit bin fill level telemetry every 5 minutes directly to central servers.
2
📊 Analyze Fill Levels
Algorithmic engine computes bin capacities, priority weightings, and overflow time predictions.
3
🚨 Identify Urgent Bins
Bins over 80% (Almost Full) & 90% (High Priority) are immediately flagged for dispatch.
4
🚛 Send Vehicles
Smart compactor trucks are dispatched via optimized GPS route navigation to targeted points.
